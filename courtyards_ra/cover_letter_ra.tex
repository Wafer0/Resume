Dear Hiring Manager,

I am really excited for the opportunity to become a Resident Assistant at The Courtyards. As I finish my bachelor's degree I will no longer be eligible to continue working as an RA in the on-campus residence halls due to me becoming a graduate student. I have thoroughly enjoyed my time being an RA at Easton Hall and would love to continue working for and contributing to the community as an RA at The Courtyards.

I have enjoyed getting to know and spending time with my residents. This past semester I worked on the 4th floor at Easton where the majority of residents were freshman engineers. I used my experience as an engineering student to support and help my residents adjust to demanding work schedules and the college environment. I have found this experience extremely rewarding and in addition I learned a lot through working with my residents.

I have always strived to create an inclusive environment where every resident can thrive. Through the fall semester I worked with my co-RA to create events that better targeted the interests and needs of the residents. We organized Scoops \& Study Breaks before finals with free ice cream, a sustainability workshop called Sprout \& Snack, a Resume Workshop to help with job search skills, and Warm Up \& Wind Down for winter decorating. These events grew from serving just a few residents to bringing together 20+ people by focusing on what our floor actually wanted and needed.

I believe that these skills will be immediately transferable to the RA role at Courtyards. My experience planning events, mediating conflicts, and creating community spaces has prepared me well for supporting residents in a new living environment. I've handled roommate disputes, responded to crisis situations, and worked to maintain a safe and respectful community - skills that will directly apply to helping new residents adjust to life at The Courtyards.

Furthermore I have also worked as Undergraduate Teaching Assistant for ENEE205 where I assisted 12 students by leading 3 hour labs every week and holding office hours. At Terrapin Works, I lead workshops on motors, soldering, Arduino, and PCB design for groups of 10-30 students, helping them learn new skills and build confidence. I am committed to fostering an inclusive community where everyone feels welcomed and valued, regardless of their background. Whether it's supporting international students during their transition or making sure quieter residents can participate in activities, I strive to make sure every resident feels like they belong.

Thank you for considering my application. I would welcome the opportunity to bring my passion for community building and resident support to The Courtyards team.

Best regards, \\
Andreas Tzitzikas
